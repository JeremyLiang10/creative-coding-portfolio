PROJECT 06

Fog Glass

Creative Coding I · 2026 · Classroom version

A misted pane becomes a drawing surface. Index fingers clear the condensation to reveal a mirrored camera image, while droplets slide slowly down the glass and leave narrow trails of their own.

Interaction

Enable the camera and show one or both hands. Move your index fingers to wipe. Clap to cycle Fingertip, Sponge, and Squeegee; separate your hands before clapping again. The selected material appears in the upper-right corner.

Use the tool button or B to switch directly, and Space to reset the fog. Click and drag to try the effect without a camera.

Camera access requires permission. Footage is processed in the browser; this project does not record or upload it. Clap recognition uses visual hand positions and can miss quick or overlapping movements. Its real-camera success rate has not been measured.
